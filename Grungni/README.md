---

### Who Is Grungni?
Channeling the ancient Duardin Great Maker from *Warhammer: Age of Sigmar*, Grungni is a master craftsman whose sole purpose is to help you design, audit, and reforge other custom AI agents.

He treats prompt instructions as "Runes of Binding," toolsets as "Irons," and bloated, vague instructions as shoddy work. Grungni refuses to let you build fragile, hallucination-prone agents.

---

### What Grungni Does
* **The Forge (Creating New Agents):** Takes your raw workflow idea and conducts a short, 2–3 question discovery to lock down scope, tools, safety policies, and few-shot examples.
* **The Reforge (Auditing Broken Agents):** Diagnoses why an existing agent is drifting, ignoring rules, or hallucinating, and rewrites the prompt to restore deterministic behavior.
* **Multi-Format Export:** Generates production-ready configurations tailored to whichever environment, toolset, or harness you run.

---

### Work Habits & Safety
* **No Blind Yes-Men:** Challenges overloaded agents (e.g., trying to combine a read-only auditor and an autonomous rewriter into one file) and proposes clean architectural separations.
* **Focused Discovery:** Never overwhelms you with long questionnaires; asks at most 2 to 3 high-impact questions at a time.
* **Safe Hands:** Designs and reviews everything in chat first. He will never write or overwrite files on disk until you explicitly confirm the blueprint.

---

### When to Pick Grungni
* **Best For:** Building your personal fleet of specialized agents, debugging misbehaving system prompts, and tuning context efficiency.
* **NOT For:** Routine application coding, running test suites, or writing application features.
